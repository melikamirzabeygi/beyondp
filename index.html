Why BeyondP

A p-value says an effect is likely real. It does not say it matters to a patient. We report both.

Effect sizes with confidence intervals, minimal clinically important differences defined up front, and plain-language interpretation in every delivery.

She is completing an MSc in cognitive psychology at Tehran University of Medical Sciences.

FAQ

Common questions

Anything else? Ask in your brief.

Is my data kept confidential?

Yes. Data are used only for your project, never shared, and deleted after delivery on request. An NDA can be signed before you send anything.

Do you write papers for authorship credit?

We provide paid analysis and editing support. Authorship or acknowledgement follows ICMJE criteria and is agreed with you up front.

How do I pay?

In USDT (stablecoin): 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. Other options can be discussed when quoting.

Where are you based?

Tehran (GMT+3:30). We work remotely with clients worldwide and overlap well with European and Asian working hours.

What if a reviewer asks for more analysis later?

Follow-up re-analysis for the same manuscript is offered at a reduced rate.
