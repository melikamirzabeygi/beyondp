Process

How a project works

A clear, written process so you always know what will be delivered, when, and at what price.

  1. Initial inquiry

    Send your brief through the project form: aims, design, data description and deadline. Do not send identifiable data at this stage.

  2. Project assessment

    We review feasibility, what materials are needed and whether the request fits our expertise. If it does not, we say so.

  3. Scope definition

    We agree exactly what will be delivered: analyses, outputs, format and timeline.

  4. Proposal and quotation

    You receive a written proposal with a fixed price within 48 hours on working days.

  5. Agreement and payment

    Work starts after you accept the proposal and pay 50%. Payment is in USDT; other options can be discussed.

  6. Project execution

    We carry out the work and share progress at agreed points. Questions that need your decision are raised early.

  7. Delivery and revisions

    You receive outputs, code and a plain-language summary. The remaining 50% is due on delivery. One revision round is included.

Communication

By email or Telegram, in English or Persian. Calls can be arranged for consultations. Working hours overlap with Europe and Asia.

Deadlines

Timelines are agreed in the proposal and start once data and materials are complete. Rush delivery under 72 hours adds 30%.

Revisions

One revision round within the agreed scope is included. New analyses or scope changes are quoted before they begin.

Your responsibilities

  • Ethics approval and consent for the data you share
  • De-identifying data before sending
  • Final decisions on interpretation and submission
