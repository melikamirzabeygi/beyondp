Services and starting prices

Statistical Analysis

from $250 per project

Rigorous analysis of your dataset, from cleaning to a written Results section that matches your tables exactly.

Request a Quote →

Typical problems

  • You have collected data but are unsure which tests fit your design
  • A supervisor or co-author asked for publication-ready tables
  • Assumptions, missing data or outliers need to be checked and reported

Approaches

  • Descriptive statistics and data screening
  • t-tests, ANOVA/ANCOVA, χ² and non-parametric alternatives
  • Correlation and linear or logistic regression
  • Missing-data assessment and reporting
  • Effect sizes with confidence intervals

Deliverables

  • Cleaned analysis dataset and annotated syntax (SPSS or R)
  • Publication-ready tables and figures (APA or journal style)
  • Written Results section
  • Short plain-language summary of findings

What we need from you

  • De-identified dataset (SPSS, Excel, CSV)
  • Research questions or hypotheses
  • Codebook or questionnaire
  • Target journal, if known

Typical timeline

Typically 5–10 working days after the scope is agreed, depending on dataset size.

Advanced Statistical Modelling

from $500 per project

Models for complex or repeated-measures data, and prediction models with honest validation.

Request a Quote →

Typical problems

  • Repeated measurements or clustered data (sites, therapists, schools)
  • You want to predict an outcome and need a model that holds up on new data
  • A reviewer questioned overfitting or model choice

Approaches

  • Mixed-effects (multilevel) and longitudinal models
  • Mediation and moderation analysis
  • Machine-learning prediction (e.g., random forest) with cross-validation and held-out testing
  • Clustering and trajectory analysis
  • Model diagnostics and validation reporting

Deliverables

  • Model specification and justification
  • Reproducible code (R or Python)
  • Results tables, model-performance metrics and figures
  • Methods and Results text for the manuscript

What we need from you

  • De-identified dataset with variable descriptions
  • Outcome and predictor definitions
  • Any previous analysis or reviewer comments

Typical timeline

Typically 2–3 weeks after the scope is agreed.

Scientific Manuscript Support

$0.05 per word (editing)

Scientific and language editing by a researcher, so the paper is clear, consistent and ready to submit.

Request a Quote →

Typical problems

  • The manuscript reads unevenly or is over the word limit
  • Numbers in the text, tables and abstract do not match
  • You need the Methods and Results written to reporting standards

Approaches

  • Scientific and language editing
  • Structure and flow review
  • Statistical consistency check across text, tables and abstract
  • Reporting-guideline checks (CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA)
  • Journal and reference formatting (Vancouver, APA 7)
  • Methods and Results writing — from $300 per manuscript

Deliverables

  • Tracked-changes manuscript and a clean copy
  • Comment list of issues that need author decisions
  • Publication-readiness notes

What we need from you

  • Full manuscript (Word)
  • Target journal and its author guidelines
  • Tables, figures and analysis outputs

Typical timeline

About 5 working days for a 6,000-word manuscript.
