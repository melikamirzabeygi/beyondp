Privacy notice

How we handle your information and your research data. Last updated {datetime.date.today().strftime('%B %Y')}.

What we collect

When you submit the project form we receive the details you enter: name, email, optional affiliation, research field, requested service, research stage, deadline, optional budget range and your project description. The form is delivered to our email through the third-party service FormSubmit. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

How we use it

Only to reply to your request, prepare a proposal and carry out an agreed project. We never sell or share your information for marketing.

Research data

  • Send data only after the scope is agreed, and only de-identified data.
  • Data are used only for the agreed project and are not shared with third parties.
  • Project files are deleted after delivery on request, and in any case within six months of project completion unless you ask us to keep them.
  • Client data are never published, and portfolio examples never contain participant data.

Your responsibilities

You remain responsible for ethics approval, participant consent and any permissions needed to share data with an external analyst. We do not claim compliance with any specific regulation without a separate, verified assessment.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information you sent by writing to melikamirzabeygi@gmail.com.
