Research portfolio

Selected projects, described with enough detail to show the methods and with nothing that could identify participants or reveal unpublished results.

How to read this page. Each project is labelled by status: under peer review, in revision, manuscripts prepared or completed analysis. None is presented as published. Exact results are withheld until publication.
Psychiatric epidemiology · Machine learning

Predicting functional disability in psychosis

In revision
Research question
Can baseline clinical and demographic characteristics predict later functional disability (WHODAS) in people with schizophrenia-spectrum disorders, and are there distinct recovery trajectories?
Study design
Secondary analysis of an existing clinical cohort (separate from the registry below).
Data
Several hundred participants; details withheld until publication.
Analytical methods
Random-forest regression; K-means clustering of trajectories; cross-validation and held-out testing.
Software
Python, R
Outputs
Prediction model, variable-importance analysis, trajectory groups, figures and manuscript text.
Contribution
Led the response to a major revision: diagnosed overfitting (large gap between training and test performance), redesigned validation, reconciled sample counts and justified the clustering approach.
Research methodology · Digital mental health

Statistical vs. clinical significance in digital interventions for adolescent depression

Under peer review
Research question
How do trials of digital interventions for adolescent depression report clinically meaningful change alongside statistical significance, and where do the two disagree?
Study design
Narrative methodological review.
Data
Published trials; no participant data.
Analytical methods
Structured extraction of reporting practices: MCID use, effect sizes, responder analyses.
Software
Reference management, structured extraction forms
Outputs
Full manuscript under review at a Springer Nature journal.
Contribution
Methodological framing, extraction framework and writing. This review is the origin of the studio's name.
Digital biomarkers · Signal processing

Heart-rate variability and movement coupling from wearable sensors

Completed analysis
Research question
Can coupling between cardiac activity and movement, measured by wearable ECG and accelerometers, distinguish groups in a public dataset?
Study design
Secondary analysis of an open-access dataset.
Data
Public ECG + accelerometer recordings (Zenodo).
Analytical methods
Cardiac–motor coupling metrics; leave-one-out cross-validated logistic regression.
Software
Python
Outputs
Reproducible pipeline, five figures and a submission-ready manuscript.
Contribution
Designed and executed the full computational pipeline and reporting.

Study design

Data

Analytical methods
Regression and mediation analyses.
Software
SPSS, R
Outputs
Two English-language manuscripts prepared.
Contribution
Data management, analysis and manuscript preparation as part of the registry team.
