About

Founded by a research methodologist

BeyondP Research Studio is a small, specialised consulting studio for researchers who care about methods as much as results.

Professional introduction

BeyondP was founded by Melika Mirzabeygi. For four years she worked as a research expert at the Psychosis Research Center of the University of Social Welfare and Rehabilitation Sciences (USWR) in Tehran, contributing to registry research, prediction modelling and methodological reviews.

Academic background

  • MSc in Cognitive Psychology, Tehran University of Medical Sciences (in progress)
  • BSc, University of Social Welfare and Rehabilitation Sciences

Research experience

  • Multicentre first-episode psychosis registry across three sites (N≈700) with a European academic partner
  • Machine-learning prediction of functional outcomes in psychosis
  • Wearable heart-rate variability and accelerometry analysis
  • Methodological review of statistical versus clinical significance in adolescent digital-depression trials
  • Video-based behavioural measures and emotion recognition in clinical high-risk groups

Technical skills

SPSS, R and Python for analysis; reporting to CONSORT, STROBE and PRISMA; Vancouver and APA 7 referencing; reproducible analysis workflows.

Research philosophy

A result is only useful if it is meaningful, not just significant. Every analysis reports effect sizes and uncertainty, states its assumptions and is written so that a non-statistician can follow it.

Ethical principles

  • No fabrication, falsification or selective reporting of data or results.
  • Confidentiality: client data used only for the agreed project.
  • Clients remain responsible for ethics approval, consent and data-sharing permissions.
  • Authorship and acknowledgement follow ICMJE criteria.
